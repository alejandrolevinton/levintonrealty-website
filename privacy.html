Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information Levinton Realty (“we” or “us”) collects through levintonrealty.com, how we use it and the choices you have. It applies to this website and to the messages you send us through it.

Information you give us

We only collect what you choose to send us:

  • Start your search form: your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number (optional) and what you’re looking for: whether you want to buy, lease or invest, the property type, area, budget, timeline and any other details you add.
  • Contact form: your name, email address, phone number (optional), what you’re interested in, your message and the language you’d like us to reply in.
  • Email and phone: anything you include when you email or call us directly.

Information collected automatically

This site doesn’t use analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels, and it doesn’t set cookies of its own. Like most websites, it relies on a few outside services that receive basic technical information when you visit:

  • Netlify hosts the site and processes our forms. Its servers record standard request data, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to run and secure the site.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site’s typefaces. Your browser connects to Google to load them, which shares your IP address and browser details with Google.
  • The Get directions link opens Google Maps, which is covered by Google’s own privacy policy.

How we use your information

  • To answer your questions and get back to you in the language you choose.
  • To send you properties and information that match what you asked for.
  • To provide the brokerage, development and acquisition services you request.
  • To keep business records and meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We don’t sell or rent your personal information, and we don’t share it with others for their own marketing.

When we share information

  • With service providers that help us run the site and our business, such as Netlify and our email provider, only as needed for them to provide their services.
  • With other parties to a transaction you ask us to help with, such as cooperating agents, lenders, title companies or attorneys, when needed to move it forward.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our clients or the public.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it to respond to you and provide our services, and longer when the law requires us to keep records of real estate transactions.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you the information we have about you, correct it, delete it or stop contacting you. Email info@levintonrealty.com or call (786) 567-7607. We may need to keep some records when the law requires it.

Security

The site uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and only the people who need your information can access it. No way of sending or storing data online is completely secure, so please don’t send sensitive financial details, such as bank account numbers, through our forms.

Children

This site is meant for adults. We don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll change the date at the top of this page.
